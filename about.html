Hi, I'm Ronny Mwenda 👋

I'm a frontend web developer, mathematics teacher, and aspiring software engineer from Embu County, Kenya. Most people online know me as "Ronny Best" — a name I've built my digital presence around, especially through my mathematics tutoring work.

I'm currently pursuing a Bachelor of Education (Science) at the University of Embu, majoring in Computer Science and Mathematics. My academic path reflects who I am: someone who sits at the intersection of technology and education, passionate about both.

I believe that clean, well-built websites and quality mathematics education can both change lives — especially in a region like Embu County where access to digital resources and skilled STEM educators is still growing. Everything I build and teach is guided by that belief.
