01 Introduction

Welcome to ronnymwenda.netlify.app (the "Website"), operated by Ronny Mwenda ("I", "me", or "my"), a frontend web developer and mathematics teacher based in Embu County, Kenya.

This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit my website. Please read this policy carefully. If you disagree with its terms, please discontinue use of the site.

Summary: I respect your privacy. I only collect information necessary to operate this website and respond to your enquiries. I do not sell your personal data to anyone.

02 Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you use the contact form on this website, I collect:

  • Name — to address you appropriately in responses
  • Email address — to reply to your enquiry
  • Phone number — only if you voluntarily provide it
  • Message content — the details of your enquiry or request

This information is submitted through a third-party form service, Formspree, which delivers your message directly to my email inbox. See Section 5 for details.

Information Collected Automatically

This website does not currently use any visitor analytics or tracking software. I do not collect browsing behaviour, IP addresses, device information, or location data through this site at this time.

If I introduce analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) or advertising (such as Google AdSense) in the future, this policy will be updated in advance to reflect exactly what is collected and why.

03 How We Use Your Information

The information I collect through the contact form is used solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiries, messages, and service requests
  • Provide mathematics tutoring, web development, and mentorship services you have requested
  • Comply with applicable Kenyan laws and regulations

I will never use your personal information to send unsolicited commercial messages (spam), sell your data to third parties, or use it for any purpose you have not consented to.

04 Cookies and Tracking Technologies

This website does not set its own cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. The only cookies that may be present are those set by third-party services embedded in the site (such as Google Fonts or the Formspree submission service), which are necessary for those features to function.

If advertising (such as Google AdSense) is enabled on this site in the future, this section will be updated to disclose exactly which cookies are used and how to opt out, before any such cookies are set.

05 Third-Party Tools

This website currently uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Formspree — Processes and delivers contact form submissions to my email. Formspree Privacy Policy
  • Netlify — Website hosting. Netlify Privacy Policy
  • Google Search Console — Website indexing and search performance monitoring (data visible only to me, not collected from visitors directly).
  • Google Fonts — Typography library, loaded from Google's servers.
  • Iconify — Icon library loaded via CDN. No personal data is collected.

I have no control over the data collection practices of these third-party services. I encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

06 Sharing of Information

I do not sell, trade, rent, or otherwise transfer your personal information to third parties except in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service providers: Trusted third-party services used to operate this website (e.g., Netlify hosting, Formspree form delivery) under their own confidentiality and security practices.
  • Legal requirements: If required to do so by Kenyan law, a court order, or governmental authority.
  • Protection of rights: To protect the rights, property, or safety of Ronny Mwenda, website users, or the public.
  • With your consent: In any other circumstances, only with your explicit prior consent.

07 Data Retention

I retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Contact form submissions: Retained in my email inbox to allow follow-up on enquiries. You may request deletion at any time.
  • Email correspondence: Retained for the duration of our business relationship and up to 2 years after its conclusion.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting me at ronnymwenda89@gmail.com.

08 Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right of access: Request a copy of the personal data I hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure: Request deletion of your personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Right to object: Object to the processing of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to withdraw consent: Where processing is based on consent, withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me at ronnymwenda89@gmail.com. I will respond within 30 days.

09 Children's Privacy

This website is not directed to children under the age of 13. I do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information to this website, please contact me immediately at ronnymwenda89@gmail.com so I can delete that information.

For users aged 13–17 in Kenya, parental or guardian consent is encouraged before submitting personal information.

I implement reasonable technical measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These include:

However, please note that no method of data transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While I strive to protect your information, I cannot guarantee absolute security.
